ABOUT SMART OPD

Making Hospital Visits More Predictable

Smart OPD is a deep learning-based hospital queue management system designed to improve transparency in outpatient departments.

By combining waiting time prediction, consultation duration estimation and queue monitoring, the system aims to help patients plan their visits and support better hospital operations.
